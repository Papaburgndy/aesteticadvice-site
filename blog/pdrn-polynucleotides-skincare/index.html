What Are Polynucleotides and PDRN?

Polynucleotides are short chains of DNA building blocks (deoxyribonucleotides) linked together. PDRN — polydeoxyribonucleotide — is the most studied member of the family: a highly purified mixture of polynucleotide chains derived from salmon sperm DNA. Salmon DNA is structurally similar to human DNA (about 95% homology), which means the fragments are well tolerated and recognized by human skin cells as familiar biological signals.

The most famous PDRN product is Rejuran, a Korean injectable launched in 2014 that's now one of the most popular "skin booster" treatments globally — particularly for under-eye rejuvenation, scar treatment, and overall skin density. The 2026 wave is taking the same active and reformulating it for topical, at-home use.

🔬 Dermatologist Insight: PDRN sits in an unusual category — it's a biostimulator, not a filler, not a peptide, not an antioxidant. Where most skincare actives push the skin to do more, PDRN feeds the skin the raw biological signals it normally needs to regenerate itself. That's why dermatologists describe Rejuran results as "skin quality changes" rather than "wrinkle reduction" — the texture, density, and resilience improve more than any single fine line.

The Science: Why Salmon DNA?

Several mechanisms have been documented in clinical studies on PDRN:

  • A2A receptor activation: PDRN binds to adenosine A2A receptors on fibroblasts, which triggers collagen production, anti-inflammatory signaling, and improved microcirculation.
  • Salvage pathway: Polynucleotide fragments can be reused by skin cells as building blocks for their own DNA repair — supporting cellular turnover and reducing senescent cell accumulation.
  • Growth factor upregulation: PDRN increases VEGF (blood vessel formation) and FGF (fibroblast growth factor) — both essential to repairing aged, photodamaged skin.
  • Anti-inflammatory action: PDRN downregulates inflammatory cytokines, making it well-tolerated by sensitive and rosacea-prone skin.
  • Hydration improvements: Polynucleotides bind water and stimulate the skin's own hyaluronic acid production — improving plumpness and dewiness over weeks.

The clinical evidence is strongest for injectable PDRN (Rejuran), where 12-week studies consistently show measurable improvements in skin density, elasticity, and texture. Topical PDRN evidence is newer and less robust, but several 2024–2025 Korean trials show meaningful skin-quality improvements with consistent twice-daily use, particularly in the under-eye area and on photoaged skin.

Common Mistakes and Marketing Traps

  • "DNA serum" branding without PDRN listed: "Salmon DNA," "marine collagen," and "polynucleotide" all sound similar in marketing copy but are not the same. Look for PDRN, polydeoxyribonucleotide, or "salmon-derived polynucleotides" in the actual ingredient list.
  • Expecting Rejuran-level results from topicals: The injectable is much more potent because it's delivered intra-dermally. Topical PDRN is "Rejuran-adjacent maintenance," not "Rejuran replacement."
  • Stopping after 4 weeks: PDRN works on a biostimulation timeline. Real results need 8–12 weeks of consistent use. Many quit too early.
  • Using PDRN alongside benzoyl peroxide: BPO is a strong oxidizer and degrades polynucleotide chains. Use BPO in the AM and PDRN at night, or skip BPO entirely on PDRN nights.
  • Clear or transparent packaging: Polynucleotides are sensitive to UV and oxidation. Choose products in opaque or amber packaging.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are polynucleotides and PDRN in skincare?
Polynucleotides are short chains of DNA fragments. PDRN (polydeoxyribonucleotide) is the most studied type — highly purified salmon DNA fragments structurally similar to human DNA. They signal skin cells to repair, build collagen, and behave more like youthful tissue. PDRN is the active in Rejuran injectable and a growing wave of topical serums.
Do topical polynucleotides actually work?
Yes, with a clear ceiling vs. injectables. Topical PDRN improves hydration, skin density, fine lines, and radiance over 8–12 weeks. For deeper results, pair with microneedling — or get Rejuran injections at a clinic. At-home topical PDRN is real, just moderate in scale.
Are polynucleotides safe?
Pharmaceutical-grade PDRN has decades of safety data. Topical PDRN is very low-risk for most adults. Patch test if you have a known fish/salmon allergy. Pregnant and breastfeeding users should consult their dermatologist — no harm documented, but formal data is limited.
What's the difference between polynucleotides and hyaluronic acid?
HA is a humectant — instant surface hydration, effects visible in minutes but limited to upper layers. Polynucleotides are a biostimulator — they signal fibroblasts to make new collagen, elastin, and HA themselves, with effects building over weeks. They pair beautifully together.
